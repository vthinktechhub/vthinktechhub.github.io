Ways to mentor our founders.

Choose the role that fits your time. Each one comes with a clear commitment, so you know exactly what you're signing up for.

1–2 hours a month

Startup mentor

Guide one founding team through a semester: set goals together, review progress and help them past roadblocks.

1 hour a month

Office hours

Hold a one-on-one online session with any team that books you, on your area of expertise.

3–4 hours per round

Pitch panel

Join a review or demo-day panel, score pitches and give founders direct, honest feedback.

Once a semester

Expert talk

Run one talk or workshop on product, technology, funding, law or go-to-market.
